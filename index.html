About Orgami

Orgami builds clean, slide-ready org charts you can paste straight into PowerPoint. Import a CSV or build one by hand, click into any team, and export crisp images.

Your data is safe. Orgami runs entirely on your machine, in your browser — there's nothing to install, no account, no server and no third-party services. Nothing you type or import ever leaves your computer, and the page even works with no internet connection.

Sceptical? Good. Open your browser's developer tools, watch the Network tab, and build a chart — you'll see nothing sent.

Your chart is saved in your browser on this device so you don't lose work — use Start fresh to remove it.

Guide

Quick start

  1. Get your data in. On the Data tab, paste a CSV and press Use this data, open a .csv file, or build the org by hand with + Add person (Load sample restores the demo org).
  2. View a team. Hover (or Tab to) any card with a team and press View team → — that person becomes the top of the slide.
  3. Export for PowerPoint. Click Export PNG (top right) — or open its ▾ menu for SVG, a full deck and more. Every view is a ready-made 16:9 slide.

Viewing teams

View team → re-draws the slide with that person at the top. The breadcrumb above the slide shows where you are — click any step to jump there, or use ← Back or Esc to go up one level.

"+ n more roles" means a manager has more reports than fit cleanly — the rest fold into that card (click it to see everyone). Adjust the limit with Reports shown per manager on the Slide tab.

Exporting

Everything lives under the Export PNG button (top right): click it to save the current slide as a PNG in one go, or open its ▾ menu for the rest.

This slide — PNG image, or SVG (editable in PowerPoint: insert the file, then right-click it → Convert to Shape). Tick Transparent on the Slide tab to drop the title and background first.

Whole org — Deck (.pptx), one slide per team; or All teams, a PNG per team (your browser may ask to allow multiple downloads). Your data — CSV, reopen or edit later.

Deck export

Deck (.pptx) in the Export menu builds a single PowerPoint file: a title slide, the whole org, then one slide per team — same order as the chart. Every box is a native, editable PowerPoint shape, and each team is grouped so it moves as one unit.

One honest note: edits made in PowerPoint are not reflected back in Orgami — your CSV stays the source of truth.

Privacy

Your data never leaves your browser — no server, no analytics, no network requests.
